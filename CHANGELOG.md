## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-08

### Added

- Read-only directory, ZIP, TAR, TAR.GZ, and TGZ inventory with archive safety checks.
- Generic local-reference, cloud-dependency, signed-URL, zero-byte, and SHA-256 checks.
- Slack JSON, Google Takeout/Gmail MBOX, and Notion Markdown/HTML/CSV adapters.
- Streaming MBOX message/label/attachment metrics without loading the mailbox into memory.
- JSON, Markdown, offline HTML, rescue CSV, and hash-manifest evidence bundle.
- Share-safe reports with stable path and target aliases.
- Baseline comparison and manifest verification commands with automation-friendly exit codes.
- Synthetic healthy/risky exports, deterministic demo command, tests, CI, packaging, and release gates.
