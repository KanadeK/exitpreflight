# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation respectful and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific, patient, and constructive.
- Treat private export data as sensitive, even when a reporter does not.
- Prefer synthetic reproductions and redact paths, URLs, names, and message content.
- Accept correction and focus on the effect of a contribution, not the person.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, publishing private
exports, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct or privacy concerns through the private channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community.

This compact policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant 2.1 principles.
